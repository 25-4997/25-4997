About Congolese Cuisine

Congolese cuisine is full of flavor and culture. It is based on traditional ingredients like cassava, plantains, rice, fish, and meat. Meals are often shared with family and are an important part of daily life.

Popular Dishes

Fufu

A staple food made from cassava flour, usually served with soup or sauce.

Poulet Moambe

Chicken cooked in a rich palm butter sauce, one of the most famous dishes.

Liboke

Fish or meat wrapped in banana leaves and steamed with spices.
