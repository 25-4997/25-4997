About Congolese Food

Congolese cuisine is rich in culture and flavor. It includes ingredients like cassava, plantains, rice, fish, and meat. Meals are often shared with family and represent tradition and community.

Popular Dishes

Fufu

A staple food made from cassava flour.

Pondu

Cassava leaves cooked with palm oil.
